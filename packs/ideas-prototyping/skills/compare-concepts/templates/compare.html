Case study page: 3 concepts

Same content and order in every concept, so you compare only the layout and the effect. The preview is the first screen (you can scroll inside it). The full effect needs the page opened in a tab. Pick one, or say what to combine.

Concept A

Pinned phone

One large phone stays in the middle; scrolling changes the recording on its screen, step by step.

Wow effect

  • Each new recording wipes up over the previous one
  • Captions alternate left and right; the phone turns slightly toward them

Notes

  • About 7 screens of scroll
  • Simplest to roll out to every page

Concept B

Film strip

A horizontal strip of phones moves sideways as you scroll; the middle one is sharp and playing.

Wow effect

  • Full-screen interlude with a counter

Notes

Concept C

Wall to phone

Starts as a tilted wall of every screen; the camera dives into one tile, which becomes the phone.

Wow effect

  • The wall straightens and one tile lands exactly in the phone frame

Notes

  • Strongest entrance; needs many screens per project
