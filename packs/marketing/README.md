# Marketing pack

Skills for the work around a product: finding out what is true, saying it well, and launching it so you can read the result.

| Skill | Use it when | You get |
|---|---|---|
| `research-with-sources` | A decision needs facts: market, competitors, user opinions, platforms | A framed question, weighted and triangulated findings, an HTML report with a "To decide" list |
| `positioning-and-copy` | Writing or fixing headlines, landing pages, store text, ad copy | A positioning page, pillars with proof, copy without AI tells, plus `scripts/find-ai-tells.sh` |
| `ad-creatives` | Making or refreshing ads (video, UGC-style, statics) | A brief per concept, hook variants, guardrails against cheap-looking creatives |
| `aso-store-listing` | Nobody finds the app in the stores, or you are preparing a listing | Measured rankings, demand per market, one-app-many-listings structure, a change plan |
| `launch-checklist` | Before and during a launch | Measurement verified end to end, trust and rollback items, a day-by-day plan and fixed read dates |

## How these were built

From a few years of launching our own and our clients' products: TV and mobile games, a museum guide app, B2B apps and our own website. The recurring lessons are in every skill:

- Research ends in rules and decisions, not essays. Numbers come with a source and a date.
- Nothing in a store, an ad account or a live page changes before a human approves the plan.
- People judge a product by how its marketing looks. Cheap-looking AI output costs trust, so every creative goes through an anti-slop check and a human sign-off.
